The run report shows how each role ran: "Models by role" gains a **Runs as** column (number of role sessions, their hosts and endpoints, or subagent), and the timeline keeps role sessions across coordinator sessions and splits a session at each reply. `models.configured` records a role's model from its `roleHosts` host. The README documents role sessions, endpoints and their limits.
